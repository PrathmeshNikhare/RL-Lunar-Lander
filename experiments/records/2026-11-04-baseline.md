## Observations
- **Seed 42 reproduces Phase 02.** It was retrained from `74f4f36` with the
  same config and seed as the Phase 02 official run (trained from `0f125a5`).
  - Its summary statistics and per-episode CSV match Phase 02 exactly
    (mean 237.56, std 52.37, the same two −100 episodes and the same three
    truncations).
  - The `model.zip` SHA-256 differs from Phase 02's (`1f93ba1a…`), but the
    weights are identical:
    - `policy.pth`, `policy.optimizer.pth` and `pytorch_variables.pth`
      inside the two zips are byte-identical;
    - the difference lies in the zip's `data` entry (start time, the
      TensorBoard path, verbosity, wall-clock times) and in the entry
      timestamps.
    - The `model.zip` hash is therefore not a reliable fingerprint of a
      trained policy.
- **Seeds 142 and 242** ended every episode at rest on the pad
  (+100 with |x| ≤ 0.2).
- **Training-time episodes** (stochastic policy, from
  `reward_components.csv`, episodes ending at or after 900k steps) averaged
  220.1, 220.1 and 253.0 original return for seeds 42, 142 and 242. These are
  training logs, not evaluation results.
- **Videos:** 3 clips per run under `runs/baseline/<run>/videos/`. Every
  clip ended at rest (+100), with returns 231–283. **They have not been
  reviewed visually yet.**

## Limitations
- Three seeds give a rough idea of spread, not a statistical comparison. The
  cross-seed comparison is Phase 05's job.
