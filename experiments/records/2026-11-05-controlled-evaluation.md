# Experiment Record: Phase 05 — Controlled evaluation of the four reward configurations

- **Date:** 2026-11-05
- **Question:** how do the four Phase 04 reward configurations compare on the
  original task objective?
- **Runs:**
  - the 12 Phase 04 runs (4 configs × training seeds 42/142/242), trained
    from `74f4f36` with a clean tree;
  - none is missing (`comparison.json` `missing_runs: []`);
  - selected from `metadata.json` (`configs/evaluation_metrics.yaml`);
  - the interrupted run `runs/time_penalty/20261103T223642Z_seed242`
    (D-033) has no `metadata.json` and is excluded automatically.
- **Evaluation:**
  - original environment reward only, with no reward wrapper;
  - 100 deterministic episodes per run, seeds 10000–10099, identical for
    every run;
  - re-run from the clean commit `e481fdf` with per-step recording.
  - Every new `episodes.csv` is byte-identical to its Phase 04 copy in
    `results/phase04/`, so recording did not change any rollout.
- **Comparison:** `scripts/compare_runs.py` at the clean commit `6bb2bb6`
  (`comparison.json`: `dirty: false`).
  - An earlier comparison at `e481fdf` recorded `dirty: true`, because its
    own output files counted as untracked. That bug was fixed in `6bb2bb6`.
  - All three comparison runs produced byte-identical tables.
- **Metrics:** the definitions in `docs/07-metrics.md`.
  - Outcome rule: D-036. Hover diagnostic: D-037.
  - The hover thresholds were committed in `e481fdf` before any per-step
    data existed.
- **Data:**

    `run_summary.csv`, `config_summary.csv`, `training_vs_evaluation.csv`

  - The per-step trajectories behind the hover, contact and engine metrics

- **Statistics:**
  - The seed is the unit of replication, with n = 3 per configuration

  - Values are the mean ± sample std [min, max] across seeds.
  - No significance tests are reported.

## Findings

1. **Task success.**
   - Baseline, time penalty and potential shaping all succeed on the pad in
     97.0–98.7% of episodes on average across seeds (291, 296 and 295 of
     300 pooled).
   - Their mean original returns lie between 243.9 and 259.2.
   - With three seeds and seed-to-seed std of 8.6–12.5 return points, these
     three are not distinguishable here.
2. **Time penalty gives shorter episodes in this sample.**
   - Mean length is 323 steps (seeds 279–379), against 395 for the baseline
     (356–437). Time-penalty seed 42's mean includes 4 timeouts.
   - First leg contact comes earlier in every seed: steps 229–303, against
     309–399 for the baseline.
   - The seed ranges for length overlap, and three seeds are not enough to
     establish a general effect.
3. **The upright-and-slow bonus breaks the task.**
   - Success is 30.7% on average (92 of 300 pooled), and 206 of 300 episodes
     time out.
   - The seeds differ sharply:
     - seed 42 succeeds in 91 of 100;
     - seeds 142 and 242 succeed in 1 and 0.
   - Main-engine use per episode is about 2.6× the baseline's (517 against
     195 steps), mostly because the episodes are longer.
   - Per step, the main engine fires 0.56 of the time, against 0.50 for the
     baseline (pooled main-engine steps divided by pooled steps).
4. **Slow airborne flight, reported separately from success.**
   - **The pre-registered hover diagnostic (≥ 100 consecutive slow airborne
     steps) also flags ordinary slow final descents.**
     - It flags 68 of 300 baseline episodes (66 in seed 142), all of which
       end in success.
     - It flags 84 of 300 potential-shaping episodes: 83 successes and 1
       timeout.
     - In those flagged episodes the longest stretch never rises: median
       height change −0.34 to −0.35, and 0% of steps rising. It ends at
       the y = 0.10 floor of the rule, a median 36–73 steps before first
       contact. In other words, it is the final approach.
     - The diagnostic is reported unchanged and not re-tuned (D-039).
   - **The `max_hover_steps` distribution separates the bonus runs by
     duration** (descriptive, post hoc):
     - no baseline, time-penalty or potential-shaping episode exceeds 192
       steps;
     - 192 is the largest value in any baseline episode. Potential-shaping
       seed 242 also reaches exactly 192, so the split has no margin;
     - upright_slow_bonus episodes exceed it in 94, 100 and 100 of 100
       (seeds 42, 142, 242). In seed 42, 6 episodes are at or below 192.
   - **What the long stretches are differs by seed.** These figures come
     from a one-off analysis of the local trajectories: each flagged
     episode's longest stretch, with its median net height change, mean
     vertical velocity and share of rising steps.
     - **Seed 242 hovers.** It stays near spawn height: a median change of
       −0.04 over the stretch, ending at y ≈ 1.35, and rising on 42% of
       steps. This matches the hovering the project owner saw in the videos
       (Phase 04).
     - **Seed 142 sinks very slowly without landing.** It goes from y ≈ 1.35
       to about 0.40 over roughly 950 steps (mean vy −0.044), and rises on
       about 1% of steps. The project owner's viewing of the clips ("hovers
       well above surface", Phase 04) fits a descent this slow.
5. **Upright seed 42 descends very slowly while airborne, then lands.** This
   resolves the open Phase 04 question.
   - Its first leg contact comes at mean step 700 (97 episodes with
     contact), against 309–399 for the baseline seeds.
   - Its longest slow airborne stretch is a median of 398 steps, descending
     from y ≈ 0.75 to the 0.10 floor (median change −0.65).
   - After first contact, successful episodes spend a median of 37 steps
     before coming to rest, against 46 for baseline seed 42. That is derived
     from `episode_metrics.csv` (`length − first_contact_step`).
   - So the extra time is spent airborne, not loitering on the ground.
   - *Interpretation, not tested:* a prolonged slow descent earns the
     upright-and-slow bonus on most steps, so this may be a milder form of
     the same incentive.
6. **Training shaped return is not evaluation return.**
   - The upright bonus's late-training shaped totals (950–979) are the
     highest of any configuration. Its original returns are the lowest, both
     in training (43–127) and in evaluation (−124.7 to 167.7).
   - Potential shaping's shaped totals (359–447) also exceed its original
     return, by the roughly constant shaping term (Phase 04 record).
   - Time penalty's shaped totals are below its original returns.
   - Shaped totals are on different scales across configurations and are
     not compared.
   - Evaluation means exceed the late-training original means in 10 of 12
     runs. The exceptions are upright_slow_bonus seeds 142 and 242, whose
     deterministic evaluation policy does worse than its stochastic training
     policy.
   - The training numbers come from the stochastic policy over a window
     chosen after training (D-035), so this is not a like-for-like
     comparison.

## Outcome validation

- Every one of the 1,200 episodes received exactly one outcome. The
  comparison fails on any terminated episode whose final reward is not ±100,
  and none did.
- The per-run counts agree with the Phase 04 raw tallies in all 12 runs:
  - success + rest_off_pad equals the number of final +100 rewards;
  - crash + out_of_bounds equals the number of final −100 rewards;
  - timeout equals the number of truncated episodes.
- No episode left the screen sideways (out_of_bounds 0). That rule is also
  tested on real environment episodes: a constant main engine gives
  `out_of_bounds`, a no-op policy gives `crash`, and Gymnasium's heuristic
  controller gives `success` (`tests/test_metrics.py`).
- The success rule uses the body centre's final x. Baseline seed 42,
  episode 14, came to rest at x = 0.2097 and is labelled `rest_off_pad`,
  which is a borderline case.

## Limitations

- Three training seeds per configuration. The variation between seeds is
  large for upright_slow_bonus (success 0–91%), so its mean describes these
  three seeds, not the configuration in general.
- Episodes from one policy share that policy, so pooled counts overstate
  the effective sample size.
- Each reward magnitude was tested at one value only (D-032).
- **Hover diagnostic:**
  - It is project-defined. The pre-registered threshold also flags slow
    final descents in non-bonus runs: baseline 142 (66), potential shaping
    142 (29) and 242 (55), plus 2 in baseline 42.
  - The 192-step split was chosen after seeing the data, has no margin and
    is descriptive only.
  - The height and velocity figures in Findings 4 and 5 come from local
    trajectories that are not committed.
- Video review covers the upright_slow_bonus clips only (Phase 04). The
  other configurations' clips are not reviewed.
- One machine and one software stack (versions in each run's metadata).
