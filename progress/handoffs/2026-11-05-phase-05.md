Phase 05 (controlled evaluation) is implemented, run and reviewed.

  semantics and tested.

- **Phase 05 is accepted.** The user approved the commit ("commit").

Headline results (3 seeds × 100 episodes per configuration, original
reward):
- **Success, pooled:** baseline 291/300, time penalty 296/300, potential
  shaping 295/300 (not distinguishable with 3 seeds); upright_slow_bonus
  92/300, with 206 timeouts.
- **Time penalty:** shorter episodes and earlier first contact in every
  seed. This is not established as a general effect.
- **Upright-and-slow bonus:**
  - seed 242 hovers near spawn height;
  - seed 142 sinks very slowly and never lands;
  - seed 42 descends very slowly, then lands. First contact is at mean step
    700, against 309–399 for the baseline, which resolves the Phase 04
    question.
- **Pre-registered hover diagnostic:** it also flags ordinary slow final
  descents (68 baseline and 84 potential-shaping episodes). It is reported
  unchanged (D-039). The `max_hover_steps` distribution separates the bonus
  runs by duration; that comparison is post hoc and descriptive.
